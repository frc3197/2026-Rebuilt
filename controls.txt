Primary - Automatic:
	Left Y - getDriveX - Drives [TEST]
	Left X - getDriveY - Drives [TEST]
	Right X - getRightX - Rotates
	Start - getZeroGyro - Zeros Gyro
	A - getPrepareFlywheel - Sets flywheel mode to "PREPARE"
	Y - getSpindexFeedFlywheelManual - Runs flywheel to Shooting Speed
	B - getIdleFlywheel - Sets flywheel mode to "IDLE"
	Right Trigger - getShootingFlywheel - Runs flywheel to Shooting Speed, Sets turret to track hub
	Back (Two Squares) - getBackfeedManual - Runs Spindexer, intake to backfeed
	Right Stick - getIntakeSpinStop - Sets intake to idle
	POV Up - getIntakeExtendPreset - Deploy Intake
	POV Down - getIntakeRetractPreset - Retracts intake, disables spinning of intake
	Left Trigger - intakeDeployAndSpin - Deploys intake if not deployed, Spins intake
	

Foot Pedal: Flops Intake
	
Secondary - Manual Fallbacks:
	Y - getBackfeedManual - Runs Spindexer, intake to backfeed
	Right Stick - stopTrackingNew - Stops tracking anything
	A - getHoodDownManual - Forces hood down [FOR TRENCH!!!]
	POV Left - getSpoolFlywheelManual - Spools the flywheell
	Back (Two Squares) - Auto Zero Turret - Creates new Zero
	Start - Zero Turret - Zeros Turret
	Left Trigger Axis minus Right Trigger Axis - getTurretVoltageManual - Manually sets turret voltage dependant on Trigger Axis (Left - Right)
	Left Trigger / Right Trigger - getTurretManual - Controls the turret manually, left trigger turns left, right trigger turns right
	Left Bumper - getTurretPass - Sets turret to aim for passing manually
	Right Bumper - getTurretHub - Sets turret to aim for hub manually
